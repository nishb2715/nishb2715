## 🤯 ML & Systems Easter Eggs

<details>
<summary>⚖️ The "Virtual Node" Magic</summary>
In my <b>MiniKV</b> project, I used 150 virtual nodes. Why? It ensures that if one server fails, the load is spread evenly across all others rather than crushing just one neighbor. It's the secret to high availability!
</details>

<details>
<summary>💸 The "73 cents" bug that cost $440 Million</summary>
In 2012, Knight Capital's trading algorithm had a bug that caused it to buy high and sell low. <b>In 45 minutes</b>, it lost <b>$440 million</b>. Always test your loops!
</details>

<details>
<summary>🎨 GANs were invented at a bar</summary>
Ian Goodfellow coded the first GAN in 2014 after a debate with friends at a bar. He went home, coded it, and it worked on the first try. <b>Beer-driven innovation!</b> 🍺
</details>

<details>
<summary>⚡ Why training models sounds like a Jet Engine</summary>
A single matrix multiplication in a transformer might involve <b>1.7 BILLION</b> operations. Multiply that by thousands of times per batch, and your GPU becomes a space-heater. 🚀
</details>
